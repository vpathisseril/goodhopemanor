About Good Hope Manor

A Family Commitment to Quality Care Without Compromise

Good Hope Manor is a family owned and operated assisted living community in Oakland Park, Florida.

For us, assisted living is about more than providing a place to live. It is about creating an environment where residents are known, respected, supported, and treated as individuals.

Our commitment is reflected in the way we approach everyday care, our relationships with families, and the consistency provided by our experienced, long-tenured team.

Family Owned & Operated

Caring for Residents Is Personal

Being family owned and operated shapes the way we approach assisted living.

We understand that choosing a community for a loved one is a deeply personal decision. Families want to know who will be caring for their loved one, whether their concerns will be heard, and whether their family member will be treated with dignity.

We believe the answer begins with personal attention.

Our goal is to create a community where residents feel respected and comfortable and where families feel welcome to communicate with our team.

Long-Tenured Staff

Familiar Faces Make a Difference

One of Good Hope Manor's greatest strengths is our experienced staff.

Long-tenured team members bring familiarity and consistency to daily care and services. Over time, residents and caregivers develop relationships that help create comfort, trust, and a stronger sense of community.

We value the experience of our staff and the relationships they build with residents.

Our Approach to Assisted Living

Every resident comes to us with a different history, personality, routine, ability, and set of needs.

Our approach is to understand the individual and provide support based on the resident's assessment, care plan, preferences, and applicable requirements.

We strive to support:

Dignity and respect

Personal choice and individuality

Comfort and safety

Independence whenever appropriate

Meaningful social connection

Consistency in daily care and services

Communication with families and appropriate healthcare providers

A Community, Not Just a Residence

We believe quality of life matters.

Residents have opportunities to enjoy meals together, participate in activities, spend time in common areas, connect with others, and enjoy the everyday moments that make a place feel like home.

Our Commitment

Quality Care Without Compromise

Our commitment is to provide quality assisted living support in a welcoming environment while recognizing that every resident is an individual.

We will continue to focus on the things that matter most:

Respect.
Consistency.
Personal attention.
Comfort.
Community.
Quality care.

Come Meet Us

The best way to understand Good Hope Manor is to visit.

We invite prospective residents and their families to tour our community, meet our team, ask questions, and learn more about whether Good Hope Manor may be an appropriate fit.
