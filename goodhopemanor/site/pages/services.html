Care & Services

Personalized Assisted Living With Quality Care Without Compromise

At Good Hope Manor, assisted living support is designed around each resident's individual needs, preferences, abilities, and care plan.

Our goal is to provide appropriate assistance while supporting dignity, comfort, independence, and quality of life.

Personalized Assistance

Our team provides assistance with activities of daily living and personal care according to each resident's needs and care plan.

Support may include assistance with personal routines such as bathing, dressing, grooming, toileting, mobility, and other daily activities, as appropriate.

Nursing Oversight

Nursing oversight is available to support resident care within the scope of assisted living services.

Our team works to identify changing needs and coordinate appropriate services when additional healthcare support is necessary.

Clinical Care Coordination

Good Hope Manor will facilitate and assist residents in accessing healthcare services based on their individual needs and circumstances, based on the resident’s health insurance or other applicable coverage.

Medication Support

Medication-related assistance is provided according to the resident's individual needs, care plan, and applicable Florida requirements.

Medication support is provided within the scope permitted for an assisted living facility.

Mobility & Daily Support

Our team provides appropriate assistance with daily routines and mobility based on each resident's individual needs and care plan.

The goal is to promote safety, dignity, comfort, and the greatest appropriate level of independence.

Table-Service Dining & Nutrition

Meals are an important part of daily community life.

Residents enjoy table-service dining in a comfortable environment, with attention to individual dietary needs and care-plan requirements.

Dietary planning and dietitian oversight are provided when applicable and according to the needs of the community.

Housekeeping & Laundry

Routine housekeeping and laundry support help residents maintain a clean, comfortable living environment.

Our team helps with the everyday details that allow residents to focus on enjoying their home and community.

Activities & Recreation

Residents have opportunities to participate in activities designed to encourage:

Social connection

Recreation

Creativity

Conversation

Engagement

Music and entertainment

Exercise and wellness

Celebrations and special occasions

Activities are provided within the community and may vary according to resident interests, abilities, programming, and availability.

A Personalized Approach

We understand that care needs can change over time.

Our team communicates with residents, families, responsible parties, and appropriate healthcare providers as needed to help ensure that services remain appropriate to the resident's individual circumstances.

Important Information About Services

Good Hope Manor is an assisted living facility, not a nursing home or skilled nursing facility.

Services are based on each resident's individual needs, assessment, care plan, applicable licensing requirements, and service availability.

Good Hope Manor does not represent that every service described on this website is appropriate or available for every resident.

Additional clinical, medical, therapeutic, hospice, or specialized services may involve outside providers and are subject to applicable provider requirements and health insurance coverage.

Admission and continued residency are subject to assessment and the facility's ability to safely and appropriately meet the resident's needs.
