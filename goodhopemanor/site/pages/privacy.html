Privacy Policy

Good Hope Manor respects the privacy of website visitors.

Website and Staff Portal Information

This website provides general information about Good Hope Manor. The restricted staff portal collects staff account details, records administrative actions, and uses an essential session cookie for signed-in users.

Authorized staff can publish facility documents for visitors. Only approved, current, non-archived documents are intended to be accessible to guests; unapproved documents are stored in a restricted area.

Appropriate Documents

Please do not upload residents’ private health records, financial records, identity documents, or other confidential personal information to the public Documents area. Contact Good Hope Manor directly about sensitive resident information.

Privacy Requests

For questions concerning website data or administrative records, please call (954) 677-2900 or email info@goodhopemanor.com. Do not use this website for emergencies.
