Referral Partners

A Trusted Community for Assisted Living Referrals

Good Hope Manor welcomes opportunities to work collaboratively with families and community professionals who are helping an older adult explore assisted living.

We understand that transitions in care can involve many people. Our goal is to communicate clearly, respond respectfully, and help families understand whether Good Hope Manor may be an appropriate fit.

Our Community Referral Relationships

We welcome appropriate conversations and referrals from:

Local hospitals

Rehabilitation and skilled nursing facilities

Healthcare professionals

Social workers

Case managers

Discharge planners

Community centers

Senior organizations

Community partners

Residents' families and responsible parties

We are especially grateful for the families who recommend Good Hope Manor through their own personal experiences.

Supporting Care Transitions

When a family is considering assisted living following hospitalization, rehabilitation, or a change in living circumstances, our team can discuss the resident's needs and the services available at Good Hope Manor.

Each prospective resident is considered individually.

Admission is based on the resident's needs, assessment information, care requirements, applicable regulations, and Good Hope Manor's ability to safely and appropriately meet those needs.

A Straightforward Approach

We believe referral relationships are built on communication and trust.

Our goal is to provide families and professionals with accurate information about our community, our services, and the type of assisted living support we provide.

We welcome the opportunity to answer questions and help determine whether a prospective resident may be an appropriate fit.

Thank You for Trusting Us

A referral is a meaningful expression of trust.
