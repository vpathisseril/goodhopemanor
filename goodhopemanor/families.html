For Families

Choosing Assisted Living Is a Big Decision

When a loved one begins to need more help with everyday life, families often have many questions.

You may be wondering:

Is it time for assisted living?
What level of support does my loved one need?
What should I look for when touring a community?
How do I know whether a facility is the right fit?

You do not have to figure everything out at once.

Our team is available to listen, answer questions, and help families understand the assisted living environment and the services available at Good Hope Manor.

When Families Begin Considering Assisted Living

Families may begin exploring assisted living when a loved one needs increasing support with everyday activities, personal care, medication-related assistance, mobility, meals, household responsibilities, or supervision.

Every situation is different.

The appropriate setting depends on the individual's needs, assessment, care plan, health status, preferences, and the facility's ability to safely and appropriately meet those needs.

What Should I Ask During a Tour?

When visiting an assisted living community, consider asking:

What services are included?

What types of assistance are available?

How are individual care needs assessed?

How are families kept informed?

What is the daily activity schedule?

How are meals and dietary needs addressed?

What medication support is available?

What nursing oversight is available?

How are outside healthcare services coordinated?

What happens if a resident's needs change?

What are the accommodation and living arrangements?

What are the costs and payment options?

What are the admission requirements?

We encourage families to ask questions and take time to determine whether a community is an appropriate fit.

Preparing for a Move

Moving into assisted living can feel overwhelming.

Families can make the transition easier by planning ahead and bringing familiar items that help create a sense of home.

Depending on the resident's needs and the facility's requirements, families may wish to consider:

Comfortable clothing

Personal toiletries

Favorite photographs

Familiar personal items

Appropriate footwear

Important documents

Current medication information

Healthcare provider information

Emergency contact information

The admissions team can provide additional information about what is appropriate to bring.

Assisted Living and Nursing Homes Are Different

Assisted living and nursing facilities serve different purposes and have different licensing requirements.

Good Hope Manor is an assisted living facility. We provide assisted living support within the scope of our license and services.

When a person's needs exceed what an assisted living facility can safely and appropriately provide, the family and appropriate healthcare professionals may need to consider another level or setting of care.

Our goal is to help families understand the services available and determine whether Good Hope Manor is an appropriate fit.

From Hospital or Rehabilitation to Assisted Living

Families sometimes begin considering assisted living following a hospitalization or rehabilitation stay.

Good Hope Manor welcomes appropriate conversations with families, healthcare professionals, rehabilitation providers, discharge planners, case managers, and social workers.

Each prospective resident is considered individually based on current needs, assessment information, care requirements, and the facility's ability to safely meet those needs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Good Hope Manor a nursing home?

No. Good Hope Manor is an assisted living facility. Our services are provided within the scope of assisted living licensure.

Does Good Hope Manor provide nursing services?

Good Hope Manor provides nursing oversight within the scope of assisted living services. Additional clinical services may involve outside healthcare providers.

Does Good Hope Manor provide medication support?

Medication-related assistance may be provided according to the resident's needs, care plan, and applicable Florida requirements.

Are meals provided?

Yes. Good Hope Manor provides table-service dining as part of community life. Dietary needs are addressed according to individual requirements and care plans.

What activities are available?

Activities are offered within the community and may include social activities, games, arts and crafts, music, entertainment, exercise, celebrations, and other resident-centered opportunities.

Can my loved one receive outside healthcare services?

Appropriate outside healthcare and clinical services may be coordinated based on the resident's individual needs, care plan, provider requirements, and applicable coverage.

Can I tour Good Hope Manor?

We welcome prospective residents and families to schedule a tour and learn more about our community.
